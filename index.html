About

I'm an investor at Valor Capital Group, where I lead our work in Spanish-speaking Latin America and opened the firm's Mexico City office.

Before Valor I invested in growth-stage Latin American companies at SoftBank Investment Advisers, where I was the first hire for SoftBank's early-stage Miami initiative. I started my career at Goldman Sachs in Latin America Debt Capital Markets, and spent time on the operating side in business development at DEUNA. I trained as a mechanical engineer at Boston University.

I care about startups, technology and innovation, and about the many actors it takes to build an ecosystem: funds, multilaterals, family groups, corporates, the public sector, and the founders who dare to start. I'm Venezuelan, and I've been part of the Startup Venezuela Summit since its early editions.
